Grape Valor Project

A sustainable zero-waste viticulture initiative transforming Serbian grape varieties into premium gourmet products.

About the Project

As a student of Fruit Growing and Viticulture at the University of Novi Sad, I developed this project based on practical vineyard experience and the idea of maximizing the potential of grapes through sustainable, zero-waste production.

Concept

The project combines traditional Serbian grape varieties with modern food innovation, creating a premium product line inspired by sustainability, gastronomy, and value-added processing.

Products

Grape Chutney
A gourmet sweet-sour condiment inspired by global cuisine, adapted to modern gastronomy.
Tamjanika Jam
Aromatic premium jam made from one of Serbia’s most distinctive grape varieties.
Verjus
A refined green grape juice used as a modern alternative to vinegar and lemon.
Grape Molasses (Saba)
Traditional slow-reduced grape must using Prokupac variety, with no added sugar.

Vision

My goal is to build a recognizable micro-brand that promotes Serbian grape heritage, sustainable food production, and women entrepreneurship in agrifood innovation.
