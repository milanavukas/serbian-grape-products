Grape Valor Project

A zero-waste viticulture innovation transforming Serbian grape varieties into premium gourmet products combining tradition, sustainability, and modern gastronomy.

About

Developed by a student of Fruit Growing and Viticulture at the University of Novi Sad, this project explores how grapes can be fully utilized through sustainable and innovative food production systems.

Concept

The idea combines Serbian grape heritage with modern gourmet food trends, focusing on zero-waste production and value-added products for both local and international markets.

Products

Grape Chutney

Sweet-sour gourmet condiment inspired by global cuisine.

Tamjanika Jam

Aromatic premium jam from a distinctive Serbian grape variety.

Verjus

Green grape juice used as a refined culinary alternative to vinegar or lemon.

Grape Molasses

Traditional slow-reduced grape must made from Prokupac grapes.

Vision

To build a recognizable micro brand that promotes Serbian grape heritage, sustainable food innovation, and women entrepreneurship in agrifood systems.
