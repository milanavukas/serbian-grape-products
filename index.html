About the Project

Where Tradition
Meets Innovation

Vinova was born from the conviction that nothing in the vineyard should go to waste. Developed by a student of Fruit Growing and Viticulture at the University of Novi Sad, this project reimagines the full lifecycle of the grape — from blossom to table — as a canvas for culinary creativity and sustainable enterprise.

By harnessing the unique aromatic profiles of indigenous Serbian cultivars like Prokupac and Tamjanika, we craft value-added gourmet products that celebrate local terroir while eliminating production waste entirely.

This is not just a food startup. It is a statement: that women in agrifood can lead with knowledge, creativity, and purpose.

The Concept

A Closed-Loop
Grape System

The zero-waste model treats every part of the grape as a resource. Unripe grapes yield verjus; ripe fruit becomes jam, chutney and molasses; grape must is slowly reduced into saba. Every by-product finds its place on the gourmet table — and none returns to the soil as waste.

Sustainable Viticulture

Every decision in the vineyard considers the entire production chain. No part of the harvest is considered waste — it is raw material.

Gourmet Gastronomy

Inspired by global culinary traditions, our products target fine dining establishments, specialty food retailers, and conscious consumers.

Value-Added Innovation

Transforming raw agricultural output into premium branded products — multiplying the economic value of each kilogram of grapes harvested.

Product Range

Four Expressions
of the Serbian Vine

Each product distils a different moment in the life of the grape — crafted with minimal intervention and maximum intention.

01
Prokupac · Sweet-Sour Condiment
Grape Chutney

A bold, globally inspired condiment balancing the deep fruitiness of Prokupac grapes with warming spices and tangy notes. Exceptional with aged cheeses, charcuterie, and roasted meats.

02
Tamjanika · Aromatic Premium Jam
Tamjanika Jam

Made from the distinctively floral, muscat-scented Tamjanika variety, this jam carries the unmistakable perfume of a Serbian summer vineyard. Pure, aromatic, unforgettable.

03
Unripe Grapes · Culinary Acid
Verjus

Pressed from unripe green grapes, verjus is the forgotten hero of classical cuisine. A bright, complex culinary alternative to vinegar or lemon — beloved by chefs for its depth and gentle acidity.

04
Prokupac · Slow-Reduced Must
Grape Molasses / Saba

A rich, obsidian reduction of Prokupac grape must, slowly cooked over hours until transformed into a syrup of extraordinary depth. Drizzled over desserts, stirred into sauces, or savoured alone.

The Founder
A Student of the Vine
BSc Fruit Growing & Viticulture  ·  University of Novi Sad

Growing up surrounded by the vineyards of Vojvodina, I learned early that the grape is far more than its juice. This project is the product of years of study, a deep respect for the land, and an unyielding belief that traditional knowledge and modern gastronomy can coexist beautifully.

As a woman in an industry still dominated by men, I built Vinova to show that the future of Serbian agrifood is diverse, innovative, and rooted in ecology.
